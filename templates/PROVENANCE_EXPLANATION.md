# VIPERCREST ACADEMIC COPY PROVENANCE EXPLANATION

### What is the ViperCrest Academic Copy Provenance Record?

Every authorized academic copy of ViperCrest is permanently recorded on an append-only, tamper-evident cryptographic ledger (`ledger.jsonl`). 

This record provides:
1. **Mathematical Attribution:** Verifiable proof that Copy ID `{{COPY_ID}}` was officially prepared and authorized by the Licensor.
2. **Deterministic Hash Linkage:** Each block is chained to the SHA-256 hash of its predecessor, creating a forward-secure ledger beginning at `VC-ACADEMIC-GENESIS`.
3. **Artifact Integrity:** The exact SHA-256 hash of the deliverable archive is recorded in the ledger block, preventing undetected tampering or backdoors.

### IMPORTANT REGULATORY & LEGAL CLARIFICATION:
The ViperCrest Academic Copy Provenance Record represents:
* **The verifiable issuance record of a licensed, non-commercial academic software copy.**

The Provenance Record DOES NOT represent:
* Equity, shares, or corporate stock.
* Ownership of the ViperCrest project or company.
* Profit participation, royalties, or revenue-sharing rights.
* An investment contract or security.
* A transferable financial asset or token.
* Authority to mint official successor licenses.

The ledger exists exclusively for software integrity, chain-of-custody tracking, and research provenance.
